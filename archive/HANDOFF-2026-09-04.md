## What I changed, and why

**The Magnet move was nearly free — one line per config.** The driver already
read `sorted(channels)` rather than `1..N`, and a CSV column is the channel's
*name*, never its input number. So `Magnet` is one continuous, comparable
column across the move. `tests_ltspm3/conftest.py` now runs the entire control
harness on the gapped map `{1, 2, 5}`; that gap is the point, and it is the
only standing guard that nothing downstream assumes contiguous inputs.

**One real bug, and it was in the simulator.** `Sim218` answered `KRDG? 0` from
a hardcoded three-element list, so a channel on input 5 came back `0.0000` —
which is exactly what the real box reports for a bare input, and is graded
`NO_SENSOR`. A fake that manufactures the single failure signature it exists to
help you distinguish is worse than no fake. It now derives its populated inputs
from the cryostat's own channel map, and `aux_base` gained a `default` so
"unpopulated" and "resting at 0 K" stop being the same answer. Two tests in
`tests/test_ls218.py` pin both halves.

**The Coldplate got documentation and no numbers.** This is the deliberate part:
**no correction is quoted anywhere in the repo, and none should be guessed.**
The error depends on temperature — a warped curve point is not a constant
offset — and nobody has measured it. Invariant 9 with nothing yet to win with.
What the docs do instead is mark every pre-cutover Coldplate figure as suspect
and leave a TODO to reprocess the logs onto the corrected curve.

**The log was broken at the seam on purpose.** Because the column names did not
change, restarting would have appended post-cutover rows to the same CSV and
the viewer would have drawn a straight line through a step that never
physically happened. The pre-cutover part of that day is archived to
`data/pre-recal-2026-09-04/` (with a README) so no single CSV spans two
calibrations. It is deliberately outside the recorder's directory: viewer
backfill only walks the followed file's own directory, so it will not splice.

## The finding worth following up

`Coldplate` is not a monitored passenger in `analysis/` — it is `T_c`, entering
as `Λ(T_s) = Q + Λ(T_c)`. Every fit in that directory used pre-cutover values.

More interesting than the error is what it may *explain*. `analysis/README.md`
carries a standing caveat that **at zero power the sample settles 0.79 K below
the coldplate reading**, attributed to "thermometry plus stray magnet-side
load". A sample cannot rest colder than its own heat sink; a sink reading high
is precisely that thermometry, and the correction is the same order as the
anomaly.

**That is a hypothesis, not a result** — neither has been measured against the
other. If it holds, the "model undefined below ~12 K" restriction may be
liftable and the stray magnet-side load may not exist at all. The write-up and
the order to re-run in are at the top of `analysis/README.md`. **Nothing in
`control/` depends on any of it**, so none of it is urgent or a safety matter.
